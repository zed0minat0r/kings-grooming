Our policies.

Late cancellations

If you need to cancel more than 24 hours before your appointment, just give us a call or shoot us a message. If you don't let us know or have to cancel less than 3 hours before your appointment, you may be subject to a cancellation fee at the discretion of the stylist.

Tardiness

Please arrive on time for each appointment. If you are more than 15 minutes late, we may have to reschedule your appointment for another time. If you're running late, let us know.
