VI   Parlor etiquette

Not rules. Just respect.

A couple of things that keep everyone's visit smooth, yours included.

01

Cancelling? Just let us know.

Stuff happens. Cancel more than 24 hours ahead with a call or a message. No-shows, or cancelling less than 3 hours before, may carry a fee at your barber's discretion.

02

Running late? Tell us.

Arrive on time so the next King or Queen does too. More than 15 minutes late and we may need to reschedule. A quick heads-up goes a long way.

03

Gift cards available.

Treat somebody to the experience. Ask at the front desk or give us a call to pick one up.
